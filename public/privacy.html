Privacy

Family Wealth Calculator is a personal tool for tracking a family's assets, loans, income, expenses and goals. It is built so that nobody except you, including the people who made the app, can see your data.

Where your data is kept

  • “In this browser only”: your data is stored in your browser on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • “In my Google Sheet”: your data is saved to a spreadsheet the app creates in your own Google Drive. It travels only between your browser and Google. The app has no server and no database of its own, so the app's makers never receive it.

What the app can access in your Google account

The app asks Google for one permission: access to files it creates (drive.file). It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive, and it does not read your email, contacts or profile.

Google's sign-in gives your browser a temporary access key that expires after about an hour. It is kept in memory only and is never stored or sent anywhere except to Google.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • No financial values are logged or sent to anyone other than Google (in Google Sheet mode).
  • Account numbers are not needed; please don't enter them.

Use of Google data

Data the app reads from Google (your app spreadsheet) is used only to show it back to you in the app. It is not transferred to anyone, used for advertising, or used to train models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data and access

  • In the app: Backup & data → Delete all data (removes it from the browser and, in Google Sheet mode, empties the sheet). Disconnect stops using Google and can also clear this browser.
  • In Google Drive: delete the “Family Wealth Calculator – data” spreadsheet.
  • Revoke access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions
